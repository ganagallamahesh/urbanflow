Pressure-Aware Coordination for Growing Urban Networks

UrbanFlow analyzes demand and available capacity across urban systems and recommends better alternatives before congestion and overload occur.

💡 Core Idea: "Before assigning a vehicle or recommending an urban resource, check current demand, available capacity, congestion, and possible alternatives to reduce overall system pressure."

Core Paradigm Shift

Traditional Approach
"What is best for one vehicle?"

Focuses on local individual optimization, frequently causing systemic congestion, bottlenecks, and idle delays.

VS
UrbanFlow Approach
"What decision creates the least pressure for the overall urban system?"

Evaluates live system capacity and balanced distribution to maximize overall city throughput and reliability.
